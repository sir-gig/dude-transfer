# Code of conduct

We want participation in Dude to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, accept correction gracefully,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, or sharing
someone else's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct undermines the
project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. The public
repository must publish a specific private contact before its first announced
release. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy where reasonably possible.
